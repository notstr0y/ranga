# About Ranga

Ranga is a centralized library of academic resources for students of APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU). It brings together question papers, notes, study materials and lab resources, organised by programme, scheme, semester and subject.

## Audience

KTU students looking for study material for their courses.

## Current scope

The current release (v2.0.0) covers Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) only. The catalogue is kept small and checked rather than broad and guessed.

Resources are currently added by the maintainers. Public contributions are not enabled yet.
